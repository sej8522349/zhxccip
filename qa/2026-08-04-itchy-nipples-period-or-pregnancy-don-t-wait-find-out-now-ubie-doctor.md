How can I tell if itchy nipples are due to my period or pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Itchy nipples are often linked to hormonal shifts during menstruation or pregnancy. Fluctuations in estrogen levels during these times can stretch and dry the skin around the breasts, leading to increased sensitivity and itchiness. While usually harmless, persistent itching may also signal other conditions like eczema, allergic reactions, or infections.
