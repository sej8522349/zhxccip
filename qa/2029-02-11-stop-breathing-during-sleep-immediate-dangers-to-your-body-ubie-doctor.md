When you stop breathing during sleep, your body experiences a condition called sleep apnea. This happens when the airway becomes blocked or the brain doesn't send signals to the muscles that control breathing. As a result, your body gets less oxygen, which can make you wake up briefly to start breathing again. This can happen many times during the night, leading to poor sleep quality and feeling tired during the day.
