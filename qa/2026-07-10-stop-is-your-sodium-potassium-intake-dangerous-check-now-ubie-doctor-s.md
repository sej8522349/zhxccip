Explanation

Eating the right amount of sodium and potassium is important for your health.

[Stop Is Your Sodium](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/9512538744.html)

Sodium: Most people should eat less than 2,300 milligrams of sodium each day. Eating too much sodium can make your heart work harder and can lead to high blood pressure.

Potassium: Getting enough potassium is also important. Adults should aim for around 2,600 to 3,400 milligrams each day. Potassium helps your body balance fluids and keeps your muscles and nerves working well.

Why It Matters: When you have the right balance of sodium and potassium, it can help lower your risk of heart problems and other health issues. Eating lots of fruits, vegetables, and other healthy foods can help you get enough potassium while keeping your sodium intake low.
