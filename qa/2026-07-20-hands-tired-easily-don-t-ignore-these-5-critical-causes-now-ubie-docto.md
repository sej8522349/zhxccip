Published on: 7/15/2026

What could cause my hands to get tired easily during daily activities?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Hand fatigue can occur for several reasons, including reduced grip strength, how your muscles respond to repetitive use or exercise, and muscle weakness related to underlying health conditions. Common causes include overuse, nerve compression (such as carpal tunnel syndrome), arthritis, tendonitis, poor circulation, vitamin deficiencies, and neurological or autoimmune disorders. Identifying the cause early can help prevent long-term issues and guide effective treatment.

Explanation

Feeling tired in your hands during daily activities can happen for several reasons.

Grip Strength and Fatigue: Sometimes, one hand might be stronger than the other, which can make the weaker hand tire more quickly. This could be due to natural differences in strength or how each hand is used.

Exercise and Fatigue: When you use your muscles, they can get tired, especially if they are not used to the activity. This is a normal response to exercise and can vary based on how much activity you do.

Muscle Weakness: In some cases, tiredness in the hands can be linked to muscle weakness from certain conditions. For example, some people with specific health issues may find that their muscles tire more easily.
