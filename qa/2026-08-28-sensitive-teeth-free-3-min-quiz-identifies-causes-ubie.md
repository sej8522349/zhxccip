About the Symptom

It refers to the pain or sensitivity to touch, pressure, or temperature of the teeth or gums. It can be due to infection, inflammation, dental cavities, or poor dental hygiene.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Pain when chewing / brushing teeth

Possible Causes

Generally, Sensitive teeth can be related to:

Trigeminal neuralgia is characterized by pain affecting the trigeminal nerve (carries sensation from the face to the brain). Even mild stimulation of your face, like brushing teeth or combing hair, might trigger it. Causes include contact between a blood vessel and the trigeminal nerve, which puts pressure on the nerve, nerve compression by a brain tumor, stroke, or trauma.

Doctor's Diagnostic Questions

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this symptom:

Does the pain worsen when you chew or brush your teeth?

How long have you had pain when chewing or brushing teeth?

How has the pain when chewing/brushing teeth changed since it started?
