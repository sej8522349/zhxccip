Burping, also known as belching, is the body's way of getting rid of extra air in the stomach. Sometimes, people swallow too much air, which can happen if they eat or drink too fast, chew gum, or smoke. This is called aerophagia.

Possible Causes of Frequent Burping

Swallowing Air: This can happen when eating quickly or talking while eating.

Foods and Drinks: Carbonated drinks and certain foods can cause more gas.

Medical Conditions: Conditions like acid reflux can cause burping because they make more gas in the stomach.
