Testicular torsion is a condition where the spermatic cord, which supplies blood to the testicle, twists and cuts off the blood supply. This leads to intense and sudden pain in the affected testicle. According to the references provided, the pain is often described as very severe and can come on quickly.

Key Points:

Intensity: The pain is usually very sharp and intense. However the testicle can intermittently torse and detorse; there may be waxing and waning pain.

Onset: It often starts suddenly, without any warning.

Importance:

Emergency: Testicular torsion is a medical emergency. Immediate medical attention is crucial to save the testicle and prevent complications.

If you or someone you know experiences sudden, severe testicular pain, it is important to seek medical help right away.
