Can REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) occur every night, or does it vary in frequency?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) episodes vary widely from person to person. Some individuals experience RBD every night, while others have episodes only occasionally—sometimes weeks or even months apart. Frequency can depend on factors like age, underlying neurological conditions, medications, stress, and sleep quality.
