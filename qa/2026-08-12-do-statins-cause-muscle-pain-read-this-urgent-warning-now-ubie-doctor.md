Do statins really cause muscle pain?

[Doctor's Note](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, statins can cause muscle pain, a side effect known as statin-associated muscle symptoms (SAMS). Common signs include soreness, weakness, cramping, or tenderness, often in the thighs, shoulders, or back. However, not everyone who takes statins experiences muscle pain—studies suggest only a small percentage of users are affected, and symptoms can vary in severity.
