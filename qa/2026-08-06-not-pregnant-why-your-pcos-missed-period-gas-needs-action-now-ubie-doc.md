What might cause a missed period and gassy stomach in someone with PCOS who is not pregnant?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A missed period combined with a gassy stomach in someone with PCOS is often linked to hormonal imbalances that disrupt both the menstrual cycle and digestion. PCOS can increase the risk of digestive issues like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), bloating, and gut inflammation, which may explain overlapping symptoms.

Research suggests that women with PCOS might experience symptoms similar to irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), which includes bloating and gas.
