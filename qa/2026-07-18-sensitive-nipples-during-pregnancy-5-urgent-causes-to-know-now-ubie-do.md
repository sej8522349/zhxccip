Nipple sensitivity is one of the earliest and most common signs of pregnancy. It's caused by a surge in hormones—primarily estrogen and progesterone—that increase blood flow to the breasts and stimulate the growth of milk ducts in preparation for breastfeeding. This heightened tenderness can begin as early as one to two weeks after conception and often feels like a more intense version of premenstrual breast soreness.

Because sore or sensitive nipples can also point to other causes like hormonal shifts, friction, infection, or skin conditions, it's worth getting clarity on what your body is telling you. Hormonal Changes:
