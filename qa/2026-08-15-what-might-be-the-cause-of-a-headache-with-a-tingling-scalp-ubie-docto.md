Explanation

Headaches accompanied by a tingling sensation on the scalp can be caused by various health conditions, including conditions related to nerves. For example, one possible cause is occipital neuralgia, which involves the occipital nerves located at the back of the head. This can lead to sharp pain and tingling in the scalp area. Another condition to consider is migraine with a sensory aura -- people with this condition experience headaches that are often preceded by a temporary numbness or tingling sensation in one part of their body.

Another condition that can cause scalp tingling is scalp dysesthesia, a phenomenon where the scalp experiences unusual sensations like tingling or burning without an apparent visible cause. This condition is thought to be neuropathic, meaning it is related to nerve function. The exact cause of scalp dysesthesia isn't completely understood by researchers, but scalp dysesthesia is thought to involve how nerves in the scalp send signals to the brain.

If you experience these symptoms, it's important to consult a healthcare provider for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan. They can help determine the underlying cause and recommend an appropriate management plan for you.
