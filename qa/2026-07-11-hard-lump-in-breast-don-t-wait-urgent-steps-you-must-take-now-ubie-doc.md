What should I consider if I find a hard lump in my breast?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A hard lump in the breast should always be evaluated by a healthcare provider. While many breast lumps are benign (non-cancerous) — such as cysts, fibroadenomas, or fatty tissue changes — some may require further testing to rule out more serious conditions, including breast cancer. Only a medical professional can determine the cause through a physical exam, imaging (like a mammogram or ultrasound), or biopsy.

What to Do:
