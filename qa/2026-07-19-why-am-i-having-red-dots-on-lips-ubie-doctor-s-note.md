Explanation

There are several reasons you might see red dots on your lips. Sometimes, a condition called psoriasis can affect the lips. In this case, red spots or patches may appear along with flaky, silvery skin. Other times, red dots can be a sign of a condition called discoid lupus erythematosus, which may cause red, scaly spots on the lower lip. There is also a possibility of atopic cheilitis, where the lips become inflamed and develop red spots because of an allergic reaction or sensitivity to something you touched or ate. Other factors like chapped lips, sun exposure, or even irritation from cosmetics can sometimes create these red dots. Each condition has its own signs and symptoms, so it’s important to look for other clues like pain, scaling, or changes in the skin’s texture. If these red dots continue, spread, or make you uncomfortable, talking with a healthcare provider can help find the exact cause and the right treatment for your lips.
