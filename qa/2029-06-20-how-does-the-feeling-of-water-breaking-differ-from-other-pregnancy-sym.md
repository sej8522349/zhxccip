Explanation

When a pregnant person’s water breaks, it is because the protective sac holding the baby has ruptured and amniotic fluid flows out. This event can feel very different from other pregnancy symptoms. Instead of the familiar tightening, cramping, or backache that might happen during contractions, water breaking is usually noticed as a quick, sometimes sudden, release of liquid. Some people feel a strong gush, while others may notice a slow, steady leak. This fluid is typically clear or slightly sticky, warm to the touch, and not painful in itself. In contrast, other pregnancy symptoms like contractions feel like squeezing or tightening pain and usually come in waves that build up gradually. The feeling of water breaking is unique because it signals that the membranes have ruptured, which may be a sign that labor is starting or that there has been a prelabor rupture of membranes. Always let a healthcare provider know if you think your water has broken, so that they can check and make sure everything is alright.
