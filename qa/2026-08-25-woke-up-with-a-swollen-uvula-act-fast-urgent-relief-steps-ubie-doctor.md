Waking up with a swollen uvula is often caused by an allergic reaction, viral or bacterial infection, dehydration, acid reflux, or snoring-related irritation. Common symptoms include a sore throat, gagging sensation, difficulty swallowing, and a feeling of something stuck in the throat. Seek immediate medical attention if you experience trouble breathing, severe swelling, or high fever, as these may signal a serious condition like anaphylaxis or a throat infection requiring urgent care.

Explanation

Waking up with a swollen uvula can be concerning. The uvula is the small, fleshy part that hangs at the back of your throat. A swollen uvula may be caused by an allergic reaction, known as anaphylaxis, which can be serious and requires immediate medical care. In some cases, uvulitis, or inflammation of the uvula, can partially block the airway, making it hard to breathe.
