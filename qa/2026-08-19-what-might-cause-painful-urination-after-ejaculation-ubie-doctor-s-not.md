What might cause painful urination after ejaculation?

Painful urination after ejaculation can be uncomfortable and worrying. There are a few reasons this might happen:

Prostatitis: This is when the prostate gland gets swollen or inflamed, which can cause pain during urination and ejaculation. According to research, prostatitis is a common issue that can cause these symptoms. Other infections of the urogenital tract can also cause painful urination.

Pelvic Floor Muscle Issues: Some men have trouble relaxing the muscles in their pelvic area. This can lead to pain when urinating after ejaculation. Studies show that if these muscles don't work right, it can cause discomfort.

Medications: Certain medicines can cause side effects like painful ejaculation and problems urinating. It's important to talk to a doctor if you think your medication might be causing these issues.

If you're experiencing these symptoms, it might be helpful to consult with a healthcare provider to figure out the exact cause and get the right treatment.
