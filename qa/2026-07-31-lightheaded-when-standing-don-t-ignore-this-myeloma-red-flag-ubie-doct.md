# Lightheaded When Standing? Don’t Ignore This Myeloma Red Flag | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

Could multiple myeloma be causing me to feel lightheaded when I stand up?

[Myeloma Red Flag](https://brainmessenger.xianfengchem.com/blog/5404815805.html)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Lightheadedness when standing up is not typically a direct symptom of multiple myeloma. However, multiple myeloma can cause anemia, kidney problems, or high blood calcium, all of which may indirectly lead to dizziness or lightheadedness upon standing. If you experience this symptom frequently, it's important to consider a broader range of possible causes, including dehydration, low blood pressure, medication side effects, or other underlying conditions.

Because dizziness when standing can stem from many different issues, the smartest next step is to identify what's actually driving your symptoms. Explanation

From the references given, there is no direct mention of multiple myeloma causing lightheadedness when standing. However, lightheadedness can occur due to changes in blood flow when you stand up.

[This Myeloma Red Flag](https://cortexcompass.jcesqc8.com/blog/7201697073.html)

Blood Flow and Posture: One study discusses blood flow changes in the brain when you stand up, which might cause lightheadedness. This can happen if the blood flow is not fast enough to adjust to the new position.

Other Causes: Balance problems and dizziness can happen after neck surgery, but this is different from what you asked about.

[Red Flag](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/3220102581.html)

Unrelated Conditions: Another reference talks about symptoms from a liver condition, which is not related to multiple myeloma.
