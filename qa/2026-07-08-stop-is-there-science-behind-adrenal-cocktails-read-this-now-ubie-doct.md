Adrenal cocktails are drink-based supplements marketed to support adrenal gland function, boost energy, and combat fatigue. They typically contain ingredients like vitamin C, sea salt, and potassium-rich juices (such as orange juice or coconut water). However, current scientific evidence does not support the claim that adrenal cocktails treat "adrenal fatigue," a condition not recognized by mainstream medicine. While certain ingredients may modestly influence hydration or energy levels, more rigorous research is needed to confirm any real benefit.
