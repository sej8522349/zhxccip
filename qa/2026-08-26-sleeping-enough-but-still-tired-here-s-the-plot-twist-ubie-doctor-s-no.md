Common Reasons You Feel TiredEven if you clock 7–9 hours in bed, these factors can steal your energy:

• Poor sleep quality – Frequent awakenings or light, non-restorative sleep – Disrupted sleep cycles from caffeine, alcohol or late meals• Sleep apnea or breathing issues – Brief breathing pauses that fragment sleep – Daytime drowsiness despite long hours in bed• Mental health and mood – Depression and anxiety often feature fatigue – Stress hormones can interfere with deep sleep• Medications and substances – Antihistamines, beta-blockers or some antidepressants cause drowsiness – Alcohol or sedatives can reduce restorative REM sleep• Nutritional deficiencies – Low iron, vitamin D or B-vitamin levels impact energy production – Dehydration and poor diet choices• Chronic illnesses – Diabetes, thyroid disorders, heart disease, autoimmune conditions – Infections or low-grade inflammation• Lifestyle factors – Irregular schedules, shift work, lack of exercise

If none of these ring true—or if making changes doesn't help—you might be facing a less obvious trigger.

Beyond the Usual Suspects: Hidden Liver IssuesWhen the liver struggles, you can experience fatigue even if you sleep "enough." Cirrhosis—the scarring of liver tissue—alters metabolism, hormone balance and brain function.

• Sleep and mood disturbances in cirrhosis A 2011 Journal of Hepatology study (Montagnese et al.) found that patients with cirrhosis commonly report poor sleep quality, daytime tiredness and mood swings. Even those without overt confusion can have minimal hepatic encephalopathy (MHE): subtle cognitive changes and sleep–wake cycle disruptions.• Portal hypertension and complications Research (Ripoll et al., 2005) shows that a high hepatic venous pressure gradient predicts complications like ascites and encephalopathy. These complications further worsen sleep patterns and daytime alertness.• Decompensated cirrhosis guidelines The 2018 EASL guidelines stress that muscle wasting, malnutrition and altered gut–brain signaling in decompensated cirrhosis often manifest as profound, persistent fatigue.

If your liver isn't fully healthy, you may feel drained no matter how early you hit the pillow.
