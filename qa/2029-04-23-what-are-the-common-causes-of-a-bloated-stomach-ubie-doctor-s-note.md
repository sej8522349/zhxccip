Explanation

A bloated stomach is when your tummy feels full and tight, like a balloon. This can happen for several reasons.

Common Causes:

Diet: Eating foods that produce gas, like beans or carbonated drinks, can cause bloating.

Swallowing Air: Sometimes, when we eat or drink too fast, we swallow air, which can make us feel bloated.

Digestive Issues: Conditions like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) or constipation can lead to bloating.

Other Factors:

Hormones: Some people feel bloated because of hormonal changes, especially women during their menstrual cycle.

Gut Bacteria: The bacteria in our gut can also play a role in how much gas is produced, affecting bloating.
