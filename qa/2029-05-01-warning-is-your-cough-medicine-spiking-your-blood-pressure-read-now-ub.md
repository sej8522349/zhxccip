Can cough medicine interact with blood pressure pills?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, certain cough medicines can interact with blood pressure medications, potentially reducing their effectiveness or causing blood pressure spikes. Decongestants like pseudoephedrine and phenylephrine are the most common culprits, as they narrow blood vessels and raise blood pressure. NSAIDs found in some combination cough and cold products can also interfere with blood pressure control. If you take blood pressure medication, look for cough remedies labeled "safe for high blood pressure" and consult your pharmacist before use.
