When should you worry about left rib pain being related to the heart or lungs?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Left rib pain may signal a heart or lung issue, particularly when accompanied by symptoms such as shortness of breath, chest tightness, or a clicking sound while breathing. Other possible causes include muscle strain, rib injury, costochondritis (inflammation of rib cartilage), or digestive problems like gastritis.

Left rib pain might be related to heart or lung issues if other symptoms are present. For example, a condition called pneumothorax can make a clicking noise when you breathe, and it affects the lungs. This condition may mimic heart problems due to its location and symptoms.

Additionally, chest pain can sometimes be related to heart issues, as seen in cases where people experience sudden and severe chest pain. This kind of pain may need immediate medical attention.
