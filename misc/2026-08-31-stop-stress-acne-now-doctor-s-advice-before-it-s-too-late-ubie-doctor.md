Can stress really make acne worse?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, stress can worsen acne. When you're stressed, your body releases cortisol, which triggers your skin's oil glands to produce more sebum. This excess oil, combined with stress-induced inflammation, clogs pores and creates the ideal environment for acne breakouts to develop or intensify.
