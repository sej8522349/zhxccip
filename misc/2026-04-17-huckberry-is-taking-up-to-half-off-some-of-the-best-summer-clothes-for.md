What Is the Huckberry Annual Summer Sale?

Every month, Huckberry always drops add-to-cart worthy deals to their clearance bin, but the store's annual summer sale is the store's second biggest shopping holiday behind Black Friday. And, this being Huckberry, you're not going to find dusty, out-of-season clothing. HB's summer sale is stocked with on-trend, seasonal summer menswear essentials, from Chubbies' insanely comfortable swimsuits, to Flint and Tinder's workwear-inspired shorts.

What Kind of Clothes Are on Sale?

Huckberry is known for selling workwear-inspired clothes that wouldn't look out of place in a Cohen Brothers Western movie. HB's summer sale has plenty of utilitarian duds to cop (some of our favorites: Wythe New York's button-up, Rhodes' Portland Boot), but if your style is a bit more classic, there's plenty of sleek summer offerings to choose from, like Seavees' Darby Sneaker, Far Afield's textured polo, and Wellen's Hemp Tee. If you're looking for more workout clothes, Huckberry's also slashing prices on Banks Journal's Board Shorts (which also double as great swimsuits) and Lululemon's sweat-wicking T-shirts. In other words, the Huckberry summer sale has something for every guy.

When Is the Huckberry Sale?

As mentioned, Huckberry's summer sale runs from July 20th through July 25th. So make sure to watch this space, where we'll replenishing our favorite deals in the next week.

Are There More Ways to Save?

Shockingly, yes. First time visitors who create a Huckberry account qualify for free shipping on their first order. Plus, U.S.-based members also receive free shipping for orders of $75 or more.
