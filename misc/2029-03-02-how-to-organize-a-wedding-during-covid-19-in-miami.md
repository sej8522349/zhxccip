How To Organize A Wedding During Covid-19 In Miami

Planning a wedding during the Covid-19 pandemic can certainly be a challenge, but many things can be done to help ensure an unforgettable wedding for the bride, groom, and their guests. Let’s take a closer look at how to organize a wedding in Miami.

Safety procedures to organize your Miami wedding

A key to pulling off a safe wedding during the pandemic is to keep the guestlist for every wedding event short. This will make it easier to maintain social distancing of 6 feet between each group at the reception. Fortunately, the exclusive nature of wedding parties (the groomsmen, bridesmaids, and others who have a part in the wedding and are not just spectators) lends itself well to social distancing.

Provide Covid testing at the venue

Encourage your wedding guests to get vaccinated for Covid-19, but for those who can’t or do not wish to be vaccinated, there are other solutions.

If not everyone in the group is vaccinated, or if you just want peace of mind against breakthrough infections, it may be a good idea to have everyone tested for Covid-19 before the wedding.

Onsite testing can detect an active case of the virus, whether or not the infected individual has symptoms. You can then take steps to protect your guests and prevent the virus from spreading.
