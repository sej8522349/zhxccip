# These Cable Knit Sweaters Are the Easiest Way to Look Good in the Cold

NOTHING READS laid-back elegance in cold weather quite like cable knit sweaters. A timeless knitwear style, men’s cable knit sweaters use raised, textured stitches that cross over each other to produce thick twists, braids, woven ropes, or cord-like patterns. Often made of thick fabrics like wool, cashmere, and knitted cotton, men’s cable knit sweaters will offer coziness and heat insulation. The best men’s cable knit sweaters should be versatile enough to sport on the everyday street, in the ski lodge, or even in the office.

Long before Chris Evans graced our screens in Knives Out, men’s cable knit sweaters were originally crafted for seafaring. Around 1900 these thick knits were produced on the Irish islands of Aran to keep fishermen warm at sea. Initially, the thick, encompassing patterns were intended to make the garments warmer and to protect against the elements.

Thankfully, men’s cable knit sweaters have remained a mainstay in the modern menswear wardrobe by continuing to reinvent itself without straying far from its sea-swept origins. Elvis Presley famously popularized a rebellious collar-popped fisherman knit in Jailhouse Rock in 1957. Not so long after, Steve McQueen was often photographed in his favorite cream knit throughout the ‘60s–further instilling it as a sophisticated and timeless style. And who can forget Adam Driver’s apres-ski turtleneck in House of Gucci?

Today, men’s cable knit sweaters are a one-stop shop of clothes: comfortable and stylish to wear as part of your casual fits anywhere. How to Style Men's Cable Knit Sweaters

Versatility is key to achieving a timeless yet stylish look when it comes to dressing with cable knit sweaters for men. Classic colors like navy, charcoal, or camel in cable knit sweaters will offer a sophisticated touch that works anytime, anywhere. Then, pair your cable knit sweater with jeans, chinos, and khaki slacks to strike a balance between casual and elevated, or even with dress trousers if you’re aiming for a more professional dress code. For layering men’s cable knit sweaters layering, go for a crisp button-down shirt or polo shirt for a polished aesthetic, or cop a more relaxed vibe by teaming up with a plain t-shirt. As for what to layer over a cable knit sweater, a leather or suede jacket will always work.
