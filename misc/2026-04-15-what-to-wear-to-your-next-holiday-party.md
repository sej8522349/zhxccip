# What to Wear To Your Next Holiday Party

There’s a time and a place to bust out your best best ugly sweaters—but it’s not at your company holiday party. Instead, wear something festive and stylish, like this awesome velvet blazer.

Velvet isn’t one of those things you can wear often, but in navy, you can get good mileage out of it throughout winter. It pairs well with any color (think: gray, camel, and black), and looks especially polished with a cream turtleneck and charcoal dress pants.

Opt for a pair of black dress boots instead of your go-to oxfords—it’s a subtle but stylish choice that makes the look pop.

Be strategic in your accessory choices here. A thin breast pocket wallet is a better pick than your normal bi-fold, which can give you the appearance of a lopsided butt. A cool watch and a houndstooth lapel pin add the perfect amount of detail without distracting from the blazer. Complete the sophisticated look with a wool-cashmere topcoat.

When you aren’t sipping mulled wine at fancy holiday parties, you can still wear the blazer. Try it with a white T-shirt and your favorite jeans for an evening out with friends.
