# You’re Going to Want This Awesome Watch Designed By Victor Cruz

Victor Cruz is back, baby. After rehabbing injuries for almost two years, the New York Giants wide receiver is coming in hot—and in style.

[Going to](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Cruz, perhaps best noted for his end-zone salsa celebrations, recently took his creative talents to the watch industry and co-designed a timepiece with the Swiss brand Hublot.

Related: How to Buy Your First Watch

Cruz said he wanted to incorporate both his own personal style and the culture of New York into the watch.

“I like a watch that’s clean, but has some flash to it,” Cruz said at Hublot’s Manhattan launch party for the partnership Tuesday night.

(Cruz isn’t the only Giant scoring style points off the field. Check out Odell Beckham Jr.’s athleisure line.)

He had some specific features in mind when helping to create the watch—specifically, the open-face dial, pictured below.

[Awesome Watch Designed By](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/)

“[The open-face dial] incorporates the grit and the culture of the city in the watch,” said Cruz. “And the pebble leather strap embodies not only the football, but the Giants’ blue.”

There are two limited-edition watches available (sadly, limited to those with deep pockets): the Classic Fusion Aerofusion New York Giants model in titanium, which retails for $17,800 (shown above); and the Classic Fusion Aerofusion New York Giants model in king gold, which retails for $37,200 (shown below).

The self-winding chronographs (industry speak for watches that wind themselves and can be used to time anything from sprints to touchdown dances) are made with blue rubber straps and blue stitching as a tribute to the Giants. The back of the watch features an illustration of the Manhattan skyline and a football player.

The watches are exclusively available at the Hublot Fifth Avenue Boutique in New York City, and it comes packaged in a football helmet signed by Cruz.
