# Sylvester Stallone Is Trying to Make an Edgar Allen Poe Biopic

It’s been a busy month for movies, what with all the buzz around a tough-to-call award season. And something important has been lost in the shuffle: Sylvester Stallone has begun working again on a decades-old biopic about the late writer Edgar Allan Poe.

In a short video posted to Instagram earlier this month, Stallone revealed that he’s back working on the film script, which he began more than four decades ago.

“It’s a never-ending journey, and I would hate myself if I don’t continue it at least to the best of my ability and try to see it actually come to fruition,” Stallone says in the video. “To be able to go out there and say, ‘I accomplished it. It may have taken 45 or 50 years, but it’s done.’”

Stallone has long wanted to make a film about Poe, the great 19th-century American writer, even though a film about “The Raven” and “Tell-Tale Heart” author would seemingly represent a significant change of pace for the Rocky and Rambo star.

“I keep telling my producer Avi Lerner, ‘Make Edgar Allan Poe!’” Stallone once remarked. “He says, ‘Does he have a gun?’ ‘No, he doesn’t have a gun.’ ‘Can he throw a knife?’ I say, ‘No, he writes poetry!’”

Stallone has been opening up more about the project, and in September posted a never-before-seen photo of himself in costume as the macabre writer and poet.

“The first serious screenplay I ever wrote was about the great writer Edgar Allan Poe,” he wrote in a caption. “At one time I had aspirations to play him and this was part of the costume design... But I realized I wasn’t really right for the part, so this is all that remains.”
