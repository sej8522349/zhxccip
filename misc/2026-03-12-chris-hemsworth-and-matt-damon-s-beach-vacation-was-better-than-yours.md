# Chris Hemsworth and Matt Damon's Beach Vacation Was Better Than Yours

[Beach Vacation Was Better](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Chris Hemsworth and Matt Damon proved once again that action stars and superheroes make the best of friends.

[Damon's Beach Vacation Was](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/1719936373.html)

Over the weekend, the A-list pair, along with their families, vacationed together on the shores of Hemsworth's hometown of Byron Bay, Australia. The Thor star and his wife Elsa Pataky showed off their incredibly fit bodies while playing with their kids and splashing in the warm water. Their toned physiques shouldn't be surprising, as Hemsworth and Pataky are avid advocates of working out together. They were joined in the surf by Damon and wife Lucia Barroso.

During their time at the beach, both Hemsworth and Damon were spotted covering up with rash guards to protect their bodies from the sun, perhaps taking a cue from their other Australian buddy Hugh Jackman, who always makes sure to practice safe sun time. Though they were taking precautions, Hemsworth sure seemed to love soaking up that April Australian sun.

While the pair spending so much quality time together may seem odd, Hemsworth and Damon have actually been close friends for quite some time.

“We became friends around the time I started to work, and I’ve really benefited from watching how he handles himself,” Hemsworth told GQ back in 2014. “Matt’s just a normal guy who has the movie-star thing figured out.”

Damon echoed those statements in a 2016 interview with NOVA, saying, “Hemsworth is a great friend of mine. I'm really tight with him and his wife and the kids.”

The two families have also been on numerous vacations together in the past, including in 2017 when they traveled to Monaco together for the Grand Prix Formula One race.

During this trip to Australia, Hemsworth and Pataky also brought Damon and his wife Lucia to the Bluesfest at Byron Bay, where they snapped a few pics for Instagram. In the caption of a group photo Hemsworth wrote, “@bluesfestbyronbay crew at it again!! Best festival goin round!”

A few days earlier, Damon and Lucia had a few beers with Pataky while Hemsworth was away. "Fun times with my favourite people! Missing you @chrishemsworth," Pataky wrote on Instagram.

Sorry, Ben Affleck, looks like Damon has moved on without you.
