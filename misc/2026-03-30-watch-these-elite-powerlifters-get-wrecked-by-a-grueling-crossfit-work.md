While CrossFit pro Brent Fikowski was recently in Miami for CrossFit's Wodapalooza 2020 event, he stopped by powerlifter Stefi Cohen's gym to get schooled in a powerlifting workout that included getting his knees wrapped. In a new YouTube video, he's turned the tables back on Cohen—and three other powerlifters—by putting them through an intense CrossFit workout.

The set he creates includes 20 reps of plyo push ups, followed by deck squats, and ending with Devil's press (a burpee combined with a dumbbell snatch.) "I want to get this over with," Stefi says. She faces off against a fellow powerlifter, and knocks out the routine in 8:39, flipping off the camera after she's finished. Marcus finishes in 12:26. "That was bad," he says. "Powerlifter does CrossFit. Ouch."
