# Aaliyah and Uche Had the Most Unpredictable 'Love is Blind' Story

The following story contains spoilers for the first and second batches of Love is Blind Season 5 episodes.

Just like real-life dating, Love Is Blind has given us beautiful love stories like Lauren and Cameron, but it’s also given us couples like Deepti and Shake (enough said). We’ve also seen a number of downright unpredictable love stories on the show, from Jackie and Marshall’s messy breakup to Zach and Bliss also breaking up but then getting back together during the same season.

Aaliyah and Uche are also having quite the roller coaster relationship in Season 5. The pair quickly connected in the pods, but things got rocky once Aaliyah admitted that she had once cheated on a partner. Uche was understandably upset, but he berated Aaliyah to the point where she felt uncomfortable. They were able to get over this hump, but Uche then dropped a major revelation when he disclosed that he and Lydia—who was also one of Aaliyah’s closest friend in the pods—used to date.

Aaliyah was understandably and visibly shaken by the admission, although once Uche further explained the situation, she made peace with it and said she would speak to Lydia about it as well.

Once Aaliyah brought the situation up to Lydia, (who up until now, had said nothing about their connection), she admitted that they had dated, but that she was completely over him and she supported him and Aaliyah being together. Things got weird, however, when Lydia then brought up Uche's friends, Uche's dog, and even the color of Uche’s car. She then revealed that she and Uche had slept together just three months before entering the pods.

The next day, Uche prepared to propose to Aaliyah, but once he entered the pods and she didn't respond to his greeting, producers let him know that Aaliyah had decided to leave the experiment and fly home. A producer then called Aaliyah on the phone and she and Uche had a very tense phone call, in which he yelled at her for leaving and didn't seem to understand why Lydia's admissions had caused Aaliyah to want to get away from the messy situation.

The new drop of Love Is Blind episodes were released just this week, so here's what we know about Aaliyah and Uche’s relationship now.

Are Aaliyah and Uche still together?

In the second batch of episodes, Aaliyah and Uche met up, and Aaliyah further explained why she had been so disturbed by what Lydia had said. Uche then further delved into his past relationship with Lydia, explaining that Lydia had violated his space and his privacy while they were dating, which led to him breaking up with her. However, when Aaliyah suggested that Lydia had decided to join the experiment because she knew that Uche was also going to be there, Uche dismissed that notion.

Aaliyah then expressed that she wanted to try again with Uche, but he said he was too hurt by her leaving and that he wanted to remain broken up. Uche then attended the cast meet up, where he pulled Lydia aside to speak about everything. She denied following women from his social media, and tried to explain why she had allegedly taken a photo of his driveway, but their conversation didn’t get far before she stormed away, yelling.

In the preview for the next batch of episodes, Uche can be seen talking to Milton about how he now thinks Lydia did in fact come on the show in order to be close to him. But since Uche now believes what Aaliyah told him, it's unclear if he will try to get back with her.

It's also important to note that this season was filmed almost two years ago, and the cast may have made amends since then. A few eagle-eyed viewers spotted what looks like a woman in the reflection of Uche’s sunglasses in a series of photos that he recently posted online, but some fans think it’s Aaliyah while others think it’s Lydia. Either way, we’ll have to wait and see what happens next week.
