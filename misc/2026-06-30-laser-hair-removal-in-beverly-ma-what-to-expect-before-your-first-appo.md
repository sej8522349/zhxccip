What Is Laser Hair Removal?

Laser hair removal is a non-invasive cosmetic procedure that uses laser technology to remove unwanted hair. The laser emits light energy that is absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in the hair follicles. This light energy is then converted into heat, which damages the follicles, inhibiting future hair growth. Over time, this damage leads to a reduction in hair growth, and many patients experience permanent hair loss after a series of sessions.

It’s important to note that laser hair removal is most effective on dark hair and light skin, as the laser targets the melanin in the hair. However, advancements in laser technology have made it possible for a wider range of skin tones and hair types to benefit from this treatment.

How Does Laser Hair Removal Work?

Laser hair removal works by targeting hair follicles with a concentrated beam of light. The light is absorbed by the pigment in the hair, which causes the hair follicle to heat up and become damaged. This process inhibits future hair growth and reduces the number of hair follicles in the treated area.

What to Expect Before Your First Laser Hair Removal Appointment

If you’re wondering what to expect before your first laser hair removal appointment in Beverly, MA, here are the key things to keep in mind:

Sun Exposure: Avoid sun exposure for at least two weeks before your appointment. Tanned skin can increase the risk of side effects, as the laser can target melanin in the skin as well as in the hair.

Preparing for Treatment: When you arrive at your appointment, the technician will clean the treatment area and may apply a cooling gel to soothe the skin. You’ll be given protective eyewear to shield your eyes from the laser.

Treatment Duration: The duration of the treatment will depend on the size of the area being treated. For small areas like the upper lip, the session may only take 15 minutes, while larger areas like the back or legs could take up to an hour.
