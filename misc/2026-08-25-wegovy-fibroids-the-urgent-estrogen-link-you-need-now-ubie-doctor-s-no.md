Doctor's Note: Are Your Fibroids Reacting to Wegovy? The Estrogen–Fat Connection

If you're taking Wegovy (semaglutide) for weight management and also living with uterine fibroids, you may notice shifts in your symptoms. Fibroids are benign growths in the uterus that thrive on estrogen. Because fat tissue is an important source of estrogen in people with ovaries, losing weight can change your hormonal landscape—and your fibroid symptoms. In this guide, we'll unpack the link between fat, estrogen and fibroid pain, explore what to expect when you lose weight on Wegovy, and offer practical steps you can take if you see changes in your symptoms.

How Fat Tissue and Estrogen Fuel Fibroids

Estrogen production in fatFat cells convert androgens (male hormones) into estrogen via an enzyme called aromatase. The more fat you carry, the more estrogen your body can produce outside of the ovaries.

Fibroid growth and hormone sensitivityUterine fibroids have estrogen and progesterone receptors. Higher estrogen levels can stimulate fibroid cells to grow, making fibroids larger and more symptomatic.

Weight and fibroid riskStudies show that higher body weight is linked to an increased risk of developing fibroids, and obese individuals often report more severe symptoms like heavy menstrual bleeding and pelvic pain.

By reducing fat stores, you lower one of the "extra" sources of estrogen in your body. This shift may influence the size and behavior of your fibroids.

What Is Wegovy and How Does It Work?

Wegovy (semaglutide) belongs to a class of medications called GLP-1 receptor agonists. It was originally developed to manage type 2 diabetes, but at a higher dose it's approved for chronic weight management. Here's a quick rundown:

Appetite regulationWegovy mimics a hormone (GLP-1) that signals fullness to your brain, so you naturally eat less.

Slowed gastric emptyingFood stays in your stomach longer, which helps control hunger between meals.

Progressive weight lossClinical trials show an average 15% body-weight loss over 68 weeks when combined with diet and exercise.

Wegovy and Fibroid Pain Changes: What You Might Notice
