Restless leg syndrome (RLS) is a neurological condition that causes uncomfortable sensations in the legs—often described as burning, itching, tingling, or crawling. These sensations typically worsen at rest, especially in the evening or at night, and create an irresistible urge to move the legs for relief. RLS can disrupt sleep, affect daily energy levels, and reduce overall quality of life.

Because RLS symptoms can overlap with other conditions like neuropathy, circulation issues, or muscle disorders, identifying the root cause is essential to finding the right treatment. Restless leg syndrome (RLS) is a condition that causes unpleasant feelings in the legs. These sensations are often described as burning, itching, or tingling. People with RLS feel an uncontrollable urge to move their legs to make the feelings go away.

Common Sensations

Burning: Some people feel a hot, burning sensation in their legs.

Itching: Others may experience a strong itch that doesn't go away.

Tingling: A tingling or 'pins and needles' feeling is common.
