What is Cerumen Impaction?

Also known as impacted earwax in the ear canal. In many cases, using cotton swabs to clean the ears may worsen the condition instead of improving it due to pushing the ear wax deeper into the ear canal.

Typical Symptoms of Cerumen Impaction

Difficulty hearing in one or both ears

I experience tinnitus or other noises in my ears

Ringing sounds in the ear, i.e. Tinnitus, on one side only

Significant difference in hearing difficulty between my left and right ear

Diagnostic Questions for Cerumen Impaction

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing difficulty hearing in one or both ears?

Do you experience tinnitus or other noises in your ears?

Is your ringing in just one ear?

Is there a big difference in your hearing between your left and right ear?

Treatment of Cerumen Impaction

Avoid using cotton swabs to clean the ears, as this often pushes wax deeper. Oil drops (mineral or olive) can soften the wax, allowing the ear to naturally expel it. Over-the-counter drops with peroxide derivatives may help as well. If that doesn't work, a doctor can perform simple procedures such as flushing or suctioning to remove the wax.

Q.

Ear Blocked? Why Your Ear Wax is Impacted & Medically Approved Steps

A.

A blocked, muffled ear is often from impacted ear wax; common triggers include cotton swabs, frequent earbuds or hearing aids, and narrow canals, with age making wax drier and harder. First-line, medically approved steps are softening drops (saline, mineral or baby oil, glycerin, or peroxide-based) followed by professional irrigation or manual removal if not improved, while avoiding Q-tips, ear candling, and high-pressure water; seek urgent care for severe pain, sudden hearing loss, discharge, fever, or persistent dizziness. There are several factors to consider, including who should not self irrigate, how long to try drops, and special guidance for children and older adults; see the complete answer below for details that could change your next steps.
