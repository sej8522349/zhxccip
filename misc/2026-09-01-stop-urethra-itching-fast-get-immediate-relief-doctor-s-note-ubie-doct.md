Explanation

An itchy urethra can happen for many reasons. Sometimes, it may be caused by an infection like urethritis. Urethritis happens when bacteria inflame the tube that carries urine out of the body. Other times, harsh soaps, chemicals, or even too much cleaning might cause irritation. If you're experiencing these symptoms, you can start by taking a free symptom checker test to better understand what might be causing your discomfort before seeing a doctor. A doctor can do tests, ask about your symptoms, and give you the right medicine if an infection is found. For example, if it's a gonococcal or non-gonococcal urethritis, the doctor may prescribe specific antibiotics to clear things up. Here are some steps that might help while you wait for medical care:

Keep the area clean with water.

Avoid harsh soaps or chemicals around your private parts.
