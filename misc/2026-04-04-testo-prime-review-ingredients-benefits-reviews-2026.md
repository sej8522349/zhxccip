What is testosterone?

Testosterone is a male growth hormone. It is the key driver of sexual development, controlling pubertal changes and muscle growth. Testosterone production peaks at the end of puberty, then slowly starts to decline. Women also have testosterone, but at much lower levels than men.

Why do you need more testosterone?

From the age of 30 onwards, our natural testosterone production drops by between 1-2% per year. The low fat diet craze of the last few decades has also lowered our testosterone levels. Low fat food is also low in testosterone. As a result, a lot of people don’t have enough of this hormone in their body. If your goal is to build muscle mass, then you need as much testosterone coursing through your veins as possible. It will trigger an anabolic response to your weight training, allowing you to build more muscle.
