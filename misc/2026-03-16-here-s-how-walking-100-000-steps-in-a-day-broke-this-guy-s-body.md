# Here's How Walking 100,000 Steps in a Day 'Broke' This Guy's Body

For anyone who leads a reasonably healthy, active life, hitting around 10,000 steps per day is a pretty good target. But Mark Fischbach, known to his 26 million+ YouTube subscribers as Markiplier, wanted to go bigger. Way bigger. In his latest video, the vlogger attempts to walk 100,000 steps in a single day.

"I was woefully unprepared for the amount of work that would be required to accomplish it, and the insanity of what I was trying to do in the first place," he says.

He started off the day at a decent rate, hitting his 10,000 mark by 9 a.m. On a regular day, he'd be done by now. But he kept moving, and soon found that he wasn't used to being on his feet so much. "I was always adding in steps wherever I could, no matter what I was doing, I was always moving," he says. "And this is where the psychological changes started to take place. I realized that my life was very static... for the most part, I live a pretty sedentary life. So I needed to change that."

Practically speaking, staying mobile at all times wasn't always ideal; for example, trying to keep moving while cooking and eating led to a lot more mess than expected. But there were some advantages too; he can't recall the last time he had such a productive day.

"What I didn't factor in was the sheer amount of calories I was going to burn doing this," he says. "Walking takes energy. You don't think of it very much, because it's just working, you're not doing a lot of work, but when you're doing it all day long, you are burning an astronomical amount of calories." By his own calculation, Mark was on track to burn 4,500 calories if he hit the 100,000 step mark.

Forced to walk around indoors due to bad weather, Mark powered through his steps. But by 60,000, one of his feet was killing him. He kept going, but as the day wore on, the biggest challenge became the sheer boredom of keeping moving.
