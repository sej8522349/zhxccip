Explanation

Weight loss surgery, also known as bariatric surgery, can have a positive impact on back pain and the use of medication for it. Studies show that after undergoing this surgery, many people experience less back pain. This is because losing weight can relieve pressure on the spine, which often helps reduce pain.

Reduced Back Pain: Bariatric surgery helps in lowering the rates of spinal symptoms, which can lead to less back pain and, consequently, a decreased need for pain medication.

Improved Spinal Health: The reduction in body weight after surgery alleviates stress on the spine, improving overall spinal health and comfort.

These improvements suggest that people who have weight loss surgery might need fewer medications to manage their back pain, making it a beneficial option for those struggling with obesity-related back issues.
