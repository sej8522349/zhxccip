Explanation

Fruits are a great source of flavanols, which are natural compounds that can help boost brain health. According to the references, berries, such as blueberries and strawberries, and apples are particularly high in these beneficial compounds.

[Fruits Now](https://47466.com/w0zzynr/)

Berries: Berries like blueberries and strawberries are known to be rich in flavonoids, which are a type of polyphenol. These compounds are linked to improvements in brain function, including memory and learning.

Apples: Apples also contain significant amounts of flavanols, especially in their skin. Eating apples may support brain health and help maintain cognitive function as we age.

Consuming a diet rich in flavanol-containing fruits can be a delicious and natural way to support your brain health.
