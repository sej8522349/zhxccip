# Alcohol & Antibiotics: Stop! Read This Critical Warning First | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

Is it okay to drink alcohol with antibiotics?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[Warning First](https://beautybiometrics.hbsnzj.com/)

Mixing alcohol with antibiotics is generally not recommended. While not every antibiotic reacts dangerously with alcohol, combining the two can increase side effects like nausea, dizziness, stomach upset, and drowsiness. In some cases, alcohol may also reduce how well the antibiotic works or slow your body's recovery from infection. Certain antibiotics—such as metronidazole, tinidazole, and sulfamethoxazole—can cause serious reactions when combined with alcohol, including rapid heartbeat, vomiting, and severe headaches.

Because reactions depend on the specific antibiotic, your health history, and your symptoms, it's important to understand what's really going on before making decisions about your treatment. Explanation

Drinking alcohol while taking antibiotics can sometimes cause problems. Some antibiotics can interact with alcohol, leading to unpleasant side effects like upset stomach, dizziness, or drowsiness. According to the references, while not all antibiotics interact with alcohol, some, like metronidazole and tinidazole, can cause severe reactions if mixed with alcohol.

Important Points to Consider:

Side Effects: Drinking alcohol can increase the chance of experiencing side effects from antibiotics.

Effectiveness: Alcohol might make some antibiotics less effective, meaning they might not work as well to fight infections.

[Critical Warning First](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/4562202242.html)

Specific Antibiotics: Certain antibiotics, such as metronidazole, should never be mixed with alcohol due to the risk of severe reactions.
