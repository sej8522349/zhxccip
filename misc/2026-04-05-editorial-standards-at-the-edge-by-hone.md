Prioritizing Patient Safety

At The Edge, our priority is you—the reader. Your health, safety, and well-being are at the core of everything we publish. Our goal: To empower you with information that helps you make informed decisions about your health.

Because health information can influence critical, sometimes life-altering decisions, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of accuracy and integrity. Every piece of content is backed by rigorous clinical research and expert insights. If information hasn’t been thoroughly vetted—or if it could potentially cause harm—we don’t publish it. Your trust is non-negotiable, and we earn it with every article we produce.

Citing the latest peer-reviewed studies

At The Edge, we believe transparency is key to earning your trust. That’s why we cite every source we rely on—whether it’s a direct quote, paraphrased information, or statistical data. You’ll find sources hyperlinked within articles and listed in a numbered reference section at the bottom, so you can review the original research and verify the information for yourself.

To ensure the highest level of accuracy and credibility, we follow strict research standards:

• Recent, relevant studies: We prioritize studies published within the last five years. If newer research isn’t available, we clearly note the publication date and consult experts to confirm whether it reflects current thinking.

• Primary sources: We prioritize primary, peer-reviewed, and government-backed research. This includes studies from trusted institutions like the CDC, NIH, and The Menopause Society, as well as leading medical journals such as The New England Journal of Medicine and The American Journal of Medicine.

• No conflicts of interest: We avoid studies with potential biases, such as research funded by organizations with a vested interest in the outcomes (e.g., a study claiming “potatoes cure cancer,” funded by Big Potato).

• Human-centric research: We prioritize studies conducted on human subjects. If only animal or in vitro studies are available, we explicitly state this in the article to ensure full transparency.

• Full-text access: We review and reference full-text articles whenever possible to provide the most comprehensive and accurate information.

Interview medical experts

At The Edge, we value published research—but data alone doesn’t tell the whole story. That’s why we interview physicians, clinicians, and researchers who work on the front lines of healthcare and scientific discovery. Their real-world insights help us translate complex research into practical, relevant information.

• Researchers: We speak with researchers not just to understand what their studies found, but why it matters. Their perspectives help us place individual findings within the broader context of emerging science. This is especially crucial in fields like longevity, where research is still evolving, and in women’s health, where historical data gaps persist due to the underrepresentation of women in clinical studies. When we report on new research or expert hypotheses, we’re transparent about the limitations and what remains unknown.

• Clinicians: Clinicians also play a vital role in our reporting. By speaking with healthcare professionals who treat patients every day, we gain insights into the real-world challenges and concerns that mirror what our readers are experiencing. This ensures our content isn’t just evidence-based, but also grounded in lived experiences.

Medical review by experts

Every piece of content on The Edge undergoes rigorous medical review by clinicians to ensure the information we provide is accurate, up-to-date, and aligned with the latest medical standards. This commitment to expert oversight ensures that our content is not only trustworthy but also practical and relevant to real-world health decisions.

Content Update Process

At The Edge, our commitment to accuracy doesn’t end when an article is published. We regularly review and update our content to ensure it reflects the latest scientific research, clinical guidelines, and expert insights. Every article includes a timestamp at the top, showing the date of the most recent update or review, so you always know how current the information is.

Update the latest science

Science evolves—and so does our content. During updates, we review the latest studies, medical guidelines, and expert opinions to ensure the information remains accurate, relevant, and evidence-based. If new research challenges or changes previous conclusions, we revise the article accordingly and clearly note significant updates to maintain transparency.

Consolidate and refine

Not all content ages gracefully. Sometimes, we retire articles that no longer provide value to our readers, either because the information is outdated or the topic is no longer relevant. When this happens, we ensure that any internal links to the removed content are either updated or redirected to more current, relevant resources—so you’re always guided to the best information available.

Resubmit for medical review

Every updated article undergoes the same rigorous medical review process as new content. This ensures that any changes—big or small—meet our editorial and clinical standards for accuracy, relevance, and reliability.
