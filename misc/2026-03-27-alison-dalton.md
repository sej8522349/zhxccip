About

Alison Dalton has been writing about science and medicine for over two decades for organizations like Medscape (WebMD), the Mount Sinai Health System, NYU Langone Health, TheVisualMD, and numerous nonprofits, including FAIR Health and the Foundation for a Smoke-Free World. Her aim is to make even complicated health-related topics both compelling and comprehensible, hopefully inspiring readers to make health a priority in their own lives.

Dalton has a master's degree in historic preservation from Columbia University, and she is a frequent visitor to New York City museums, galleries, and theaters. She enjoys gardening in her backyard, as well as bird-watching and running around the reservoir in Central Park.
