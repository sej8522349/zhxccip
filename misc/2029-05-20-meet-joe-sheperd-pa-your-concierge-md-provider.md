Full Bio

Joe Shepherd, MMS, PA-C, is a board-certified Physician Assistant with experience in primary care and family medicine. Originally from Macomb, Michigan, he earned his Master of Physician Assistant Studies from Grand Valley State University and is an active member of the American Academy of Physician Associates and the San Diego Society of Physician Assistants. He also contributes to clinical education as a preceptor and continues to practice family medicine in an underserved community clinic. Joe believes strong health outcomes begin with a trusted patient-provider relationship built on communication, consistency, and mutual understanding. He takes time to listen carefully, evaluate the full picture of a patient’s health, and identify factors that may be contributing to illness or limiting well-being. His approach combines evidence-based medicine with practical, individualized guidance so patients can address immediate concerns while making meaningful progress toward their long-term goals. Outside of clinical practice, Joe enjoys surfing, traveling, and spending time with his wife, Carley, and their two dogs.
