Customized shoes often turn out badly because they offer too many options. When you have different colors for the laces, soles, uppers, tongue, and monogram, what was supposed to look cool and futuristic often comes out looking like a clown shoe.

Related: The 18 Best Dress Shoes for Men

So, American heritage footwear brand Florsheim decided their custom shoe program would pair customers with self-taught artist Ben Smith to create footwear that’s both elegant and one-of-a-kind. Here’s how it works:

[Artist Will](https://ageless-peptides.china-akan.com/blog/8226917201.html)

First, you pick a shoe from the Florsheim collection. Then you speak with Smith via webcam or phone to customize any and every part of the shoe, including the sole.

[These Dress Shoes Just](https://thymicshield.dns0663.com/blog/4262932444.html)

You can describe specific designs or a more abstract concept and let Smith get creative. Then you’ll receive a digital rendering for approval before Smith hand-paints your shoes with specially formulated acrylic paint that won’t chip, fade, or wear off the leather.

[Custom Design and Hand](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/)

[Paint These](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/blog/5677745869.html)

When testing the program, I chose black wingtips and had Smith put a topographic map of my hometown—St. Louis—on the left shoe’s vamp with a corresponding map of New York City on the right (pictured above).

The Florsheim+Ben Smith collaboration will be available starting November 30 at florsheimshoes.com. Prices start at $700, depending on the complexity of the design, and your custom shoes will arrive about two to four weeks after consultation.
