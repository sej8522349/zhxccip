# Our Fashion Team Found the Best Menswear Releases of September

Welcome to MH Style Gains, where the Men’s Health Style Team finds the best new menswear releases of the month so you can look and feel your best.

Fall is finally here. If you’re in the market for some great new clothes to kick off the season, our fashion team has done the hard work for you. Led by fashion director Ted Stafford, we found September’s best clothing drops worth shopping now. The vibe? Sleek neutral garbs that look as great as they perform. We’re talking Michael Kors’s sharp wool-blend suit that’s infused with stretch so it’s easy to move in, Victorinox’s minimalist weekender bag that’s loaded with storage, and Johnston & Murphy’s classic-looking dress shoes that are fitted with incredibly cushioned insoles. And while these clothes are from some of our favorite menswear brands’ newest collections, these aren’t flash-in-the-pan pieces. They’re built to last and look great for years to come.

[Team Found the Best](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

Michael Kors Classic-Fit Wool Blend Stretch Suit

If there’s a wardrobe staple that a man should absolutely have in his closet, it’s a sharp black suit. It’s your go-to look for many formal occasions throughout the year, and with the holiday season approaching, Michael Kors has got you covered. This modern suit has a classic feel with added stretch for movement and comfort. Added bonus — it’s sold as separates, allowing for versatility with how you style it. Dress up the blazer with the pant or dress down the pant with a cashmere sweater; this suit is a quality investment for years to come.

Victorinox Werks Traveler 6.0 Weekender

Meet the ultimate go-everywhere bag. Our editors recommend Victorinox’s sleek new weekender for fall travels, the gym, and everywhere in between. We love its sharp design, with a black nylon shell that plays well with all your autumn fits. It’s also as functional as it is handsome, with a suite of compartments to stow your travel essentials, and an expanded base for extra storage. “This bag was made for all sorts of adventures,” says Stafford.

Michael Kors Slim Airsoft Performance Stretch Dress Shirt

There are some things to consider when searching for the perfect dress shirt: a premium fabric, a flattering collar, and impeccable tailoring, to name a few. Jump to this Michael Kors slim-fit, AirSoft performance dress shirt and we cover a lot of those bases. Made with a flexible stretch fabric for movement that helps to combat the often stiff feeling of a traditional dress shirt, moisture-wicking properties for those on-the-go days, and wrinkle-free fabric for fast-moving mornings, this shirt has a great fit that not only flatters the wearer but also provides ease throughout the day.

Johnston & Murphy Sullivan Plain Toe Shoe

With this modern designed dress shoe marked by simplicity, Johnston & Murphy brings you a casual yet polished staple to add to your formal uniform. Made with a removable TRUFOAM footbed for extra comfort, a perfectly rounded toe, and sleek profile, these shoes are perfect for all occasions.

Michael Kors Classic Stretch Performance Pants

You should always have a well-fitting, classic casual pant in rotation, especially as we enter the cooler months. For those busy days filled with long meetings, these Michael Kors performance-stretch pants are ideal. A relaxed fit with a tapered hem makes them a sharp and stylish must-have.

Banana Republic Italian Merino Sweater

[Team Found](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/)

Banana Republic has always been a force when it comes to the best contemporary classic fashion staples, and this tailored crewneck merino-wool sweater doesn’t disappoint. Known for its moisture-wicking, odor-fighting, and temperature-regulating properties, a merino-wool sweater is a mandatory add to your year-round essentials. Did we mention it also comes at a price you can’t beat?

Fossil Carraway Watch

If you’re looking for a watch that levels up your look, Fossil’s Carraway is an instant win. Its distinctive square-shaped case, paired with a dressy dial and brown leather strap, stands out on the wrist. Timeless style without breaking the bank? You can’t go wrong.

Michael Kors Stretch Solid Blazer

[Releases of](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/)

Amp up your everyday casual look with this Michael Kors performance stretch blazer, which is fitting for many occasions. Whether you’re attending a formal event, going to the office, or racing through the airport, this blazer provides supreme comfort and sophisticated style. A perfect, traditional two-button blazer that boasts a modern silhouette paired with front flap pockets to secure your keys and wallet, along with double-pleated vents at the back for movement, it’s an ideal combination of style and function.

[Found the Best](https://neurocurrent.china-akan.com/)

Nike Killshot 2

Inspired by the original Nike Killshot tennis shoe, the Nike Killshot 2 is a modern timeless sneaker to rock. Simple yet sporty, these sneakers have been having a moment in 2024. Paired with your favorite jeans, office chinos, or even a wool suit, you can’t miss when you’re wearing a pair.

Nordstrom 4-Pack Merino Wool Blend Dress Socks

A great pair of dress socks to complete your look is incredibly underrated; it’s almost as if the finishing touches are sometimes the most stressful. While suit season is approaching, we want to make sure you aren’t throwing on just any ol’ sock, and Nordstrom’s merino-wool blend is the perfect ribbed style to wear. Breathable, soft to touch, and currently on sale—so we suggest you act now!

Uniqlo Double Face Coat

Finding a stylish topcoat at a reasonable price isn’t an easy feat. In comes this double-face wool-blend coat from Uniqlo’s first collection by its new creative director, Clare Waight Keller. Tailored a bit oversized for layering as the weather chills, this refined casual coat is a must have.

You can check out more pieces from Clare Waight Keller’s Uniqlo collection HERE.

Buck Mason Caddy Sunglasses

Buck Mason is a brand we at Men’s Health fully endorse for their iconic menswear staples. Now venturing into the world of sunglasses, the brand has released three classic styles, all made in Italy and inspired by Americana styles from the ’50s and ’60s. Sure to elevate your look, the Caddy is our favorite at the moment, and comes in four stylish colorways.
