Naegleria fowleri, often called the "brain-eating amoeba," thrives in warm freshwater environments such as lakes, rivers, and hot springs. It grows best in high temperatures, making infections most common during hot summer months when water temperatures rise and recreational swimming increases.

If you've recently swum in warm freshwater and are experiencing symptoms like headache, fever, nausea, or a stiff neck, don't wait to find answers. Early awareness can be critical.
