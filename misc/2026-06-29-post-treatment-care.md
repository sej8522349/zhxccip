First 24–48 Hours

Apply a cold compress (10–15 minutes at a time) to reduce swelling.

Avoid strenuous exercise.

Avoid alcohol to minimize bruising.

Avoid excessive sun or heat exposure (sauna, hot yoga, steam rooms).

[Post-Treatment Care](https://healingkinetics.bbsxyxy.com/)

Do not apply makeup for at least 12 hours.

For 1 Week

Avoid facials, lasers, microneedling, or aggressive skin treatments.

Avoid dental procedures if possible.

[Post-Treatment Care](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/blog/0949815751.html)

Sleep on your back if feasible.

Continue gentle skincare.

Bruising & Swelling Tips

Arnica (topical or oral) may help reduce bruising.

Avoid blood-thinning supplements unless prescribed (fish oil, vitamin E, aspirin, ibuprofen).

Stay well hydrated.

When to Call Us

Contact the office immediately if you experience:

Increasing pain

Severe swelling

Skin discoloration (white, gray, or dark areas)

Signs of infection (warmth, fever, drainage)

Persistent firm nodules that do not improve

Important Reminder

Sculptra does not provide instant volume like traditional fillers. The initial fullness you see is from sterile water and will subside in a few days. True results appear gradually as collagen builds.
