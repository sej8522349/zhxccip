Explanation

Sleep apnea can be a serious condition, and it is important to recognize the early warning signs. One of the most common signs is loud snoring, which often happens when the airway is blocked during sleep. People with sleep apnea might also choke or gasp for air while sleeping. This can be alarming for both the person experiencing it and anyone who might be sleeping nearby.

Another sign of sleep apnea is feeling very tired during the day, even after a full night's sleep. This happens because the sleep is often interrupted, preventing the person from getting restful sleep. Other signs can include difficulty concentrating, morning headaches, and waking up with a dry mouth or sore throat.

If you notice these signs, you can use a free symptom checker to help identify potential causes and determine whether you should talk to a healthcare provider about sleep apnea and possible treatments.
