What is Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)?

Diabetic macular edema occurs when tiny bulges (microaneurysms) protrude from the vessel walls, leaking fluid and blood into the retina. This fluid can cause swelling (edema) in the central part of the retina (macula) and may lead to vision problems or blindness.

Treatment of Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)

Treatment options depend on the severity of vision loss and include anti-vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) therapy, anti-inflammatory treatment, and laser therapy. In advanced cases of diabetic macular edema, surgery may be considered.
