What are the side effects of radiation therapy for prostate cancer?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Radiation therapy for prostate cancer commonly causes side effects including fatigue, urinary problems (such as frequency, urgency, or discomfort), and bowel issues (like diarrhea or rectal irritation). Some men also experience erectile dysfunction. These side effects vary from person to person and may be short-term during treatment or develop as long-lasting concerns months or years later.

Common Side Effects

Fatigue: Many patients feel very tired during and after treatment.

Bladder Problems: This includes needing to urinate more often or experiencing pain while urinating.

Bowel Issues: Diarrhea or discomfort in the stomach area can occur.

Long-term Effects

Some side effects might last longer or show up after treatment ends. These can include continued bowel and bladder issues or changes in sexual function.
