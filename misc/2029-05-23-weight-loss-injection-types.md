# Weight Loss Injection Types

Weight Loss Injection Types

Many people struggle with weight loss. It used to be that the only options for weight loss were fad diets, extreme exercise plans that were difficult to follow, and surgery. Now, however, medical weight loss has taken many steps forward, and weight loss injections are now an important part of how many people lose weight.

hCG injections

One type of injection that is used for helping people lose weight is an injection of a particular hormone found in women during pregnancy known as human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG).

This injection works by using one of the body’s natural hormones to reset and regulate the body’s metabolism.

Lipotropic injections

Our next type of weight loss shot is sometimes called lipo injections or lipo B shots due to the utilization of different B vitamins in the formula. Lipotropic injections are injections that change the way that the body acquires and utilizes fat stored in the body and when eating food. For this reason, these injections are believed to be very effective in helping people lose weight.

These injections use a combination of vitamins, minerals, and nutrients to help improve the body’s performance, including absorption, burning fat, and baseline metabolism. Some of the common ingredients include vitamins B 6 and B 12, complex B vitamins, L-carnitine, and phentermine, among other substances.

These injections are intended to be combined with a daily exercise regimen and a strict diet to achieve optimal results. It should be noted that these injections are inconclusive in some patients, and there are certain risks involved with taking them, including side effects, such as anxiety, constipation, fatigue, numbness in the hands and feet, and increased heart rate. Other side effects can occur if you have health conditions like thyroid disease.

Always consult your doctor about the risks and any existing health problems you may have before taking one of these injections.

Prescription weight loss injections

So far, we’ve covered a few of the more popular non-prescription weight loss injections. While there is some evidence to show that they may help with weight loss, only certain prescription medications have clinically proven results.

When administered under the supervision of a weight management specialist, several medications help people lose weight and keep it off for the long term.

Among the drugs that may be prescribed for weight loss or used as part of a weight management program, two of the widely used and effective drugs are Liraglutide and Semaglutide. Both medications are administered by injection, with Liraglutide being a daily injection and Semaglutide being administered weekly.

These medications work by supplementing certain hormones in the body that regulate appetite, metabolism, and digestion to make patients feel fuller longer, be less hungry, and metabolize food more quickly, leading to proven and consistent weight loss and weight loss retention over time. As always, any type of weight loss medication should be administered with a sensible diet (not extreme dieting as with some options) and a daily exercise regimen.
