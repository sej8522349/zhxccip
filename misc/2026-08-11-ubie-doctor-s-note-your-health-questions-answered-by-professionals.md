What is a Stroke? Why Your Brain is Failing & Medically Approved Next Steps

A stroke happens when blood flow to the brain is suddenly cut off, killing brain cells within minutes. Most strokes are ischemic (caused by a clot), while fewer are hemorrhagic (caused by bleeding). Transient ischemic attacks (TIAs) act as brief warning events. Key stroke symptoms include face drooping, arm weakness, and speech difficulty — all signs of a medical emergency. Call 911 immediately. Fast treatment enables clot-busting medication, thrombectomy, bleeding control, and early rehabilitation. Prevention centers on managing blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, and atrial fibrillation, plus quitting smoking, exercising, and eating a heart-healthy diet. What Is ALS? Why Your Muscles Fail and Medically Approved Next Steps
