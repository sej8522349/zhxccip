About this lesson

Revolax® Lip Filler Training focuses on enhancing natural lip hydration and improving perioral skin texture with a subtle gloss effect. In this practical session, Dr. Juan Sopena demonstrates safe and precise cannula techniques using Revolax® Deep, targeting the submucosal layer to restore softness without adding volume.

You’ll learn how to achieve balanced lip proportions, prevent early barcode lines, and refine surface texture while maintaining a youthful, natural look. This lesson emphasizes anatomical accuracy, product control, and molding for consistent, smooth results.
